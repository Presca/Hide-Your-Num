41729 Hide Your Num

Everyone wears a number on their forehead. Read theirs and type it before they read yours.

How to play
  • Move WASD, jump Space, look mouse. Click the game to grab the mouse, Esc releases it.
  • Eliminate: look at someone's face and type their 5 digits. A wrong code locks your keys for a moment.
  • You can only read a number you can actually see: in range, in front of you, no cover, no smoke, and the face turned towards you.
  • Gadgets: Q smoke (hides numbers inside), E flash (blinds people in front of you), F shuffle (new number for you).
  • Tab scoreboard. Most eliminations when the clock runs out wins the round.
Desktop with keyboard and mouse. Inspired by Figure Me Out.
